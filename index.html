FAQ

Common questions

Is McGill Institute Australia part of McGill University in Canada?

No. McGill Institute Australia is an independent registered training organisation in Parramatta, NSW, and is not affiliated with McGill University in Canada.

What do RTO 45906 and CRICOS 03678G mean?

RTO 45906 is our registered training organisation number, confirming we deliver nationally recognised qualifications. CRICOS 03678G is our registration to enrol international students.

Can international students study here?

Yes. As a CRICOS-registered provider, we welcome eligible international students, and we work with education agents to support enrolment.

How much do courses cost?

Fees vary by course and are confirmed directly with our enrolment team. Submit an enquiry and we'll send you current pricing.

When can I start?

Intake dates vary by course. Enquire and our team will confirm the next available start date for your chosen course.

Do I need to meet entry requirements?

Entry requirements differ by course and student type (domestic or international). Our team will confirm what applies to you during your enquiry.

How do I enrol?

Submit an enquiry below with your course of interest. Our team will guide you through entry requirements, fees and enrolment from there.

What is a Subclass 500 student visa?

The Subclass 500 is Australia's standard student visa, allowing international students to study full-time at a CRICOS-registered provider like McGill Institute Australia. It generally requires a Confirmation of Enrolment, evidence you meet the Genuine Student requirement, proof of financial capacity, and health cover. This is general information only, not migration advice. A registered migration agent or the Department of Home Affairs can confirm requirements for your situation.

What is Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)?

OSHC is health insurance required for most student visa holders for the length of their visa, covering basic medical and hospital costs while studying in Australia. It can be arranged through an approved OSHC provider either independently or, in some cases, through your education provider. Ask our admissions team what applies to your enrolment.

Where can I find accommodation near the Parramatta campus?

We don't operate student accommodation directly. Parramatta has a range of rental and share-housing options in the surrounding suburbs; we'd recommend starting your housing search as early as possible once your enrolment is confirmed. Our admissions team can point you toward general resources during your enquiry.

How do I get to campus from Parramatta or Harris Park station?

Our campus at Level 1, 30 Cowper St is within walking distance of both Parramatta and Harris Park stations on the Sydney Trains network. See the Campus & Location map above for exact directions.

Not sure which stream or entry pathway fits your goals?

Speak directly with our Parramatta team to confirm current fees, intake dates, and visa requirements for international students.
