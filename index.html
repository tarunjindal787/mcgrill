The Register

Seven registered courses

Every course below is part of McGill Institute's registered scope under RTO 45906. Filter by discipline.

PLATE NO. 01

Certificate III in Engineering
– Fabrication Trade

A hands-on trade qualification in metal fabrication, covering workshop safety, reading technical drawings, and fabrication techniques used across manufacturing and construction.

Discipline: Engineering

PLATE NO. 02

Certificate IV in Kitchen Management

Trains cooks moving into kitchen supervision: food safety systems, menu and cost control, and leading a kitchen team in a commercial setting.

Discipline: Hospitality

PLATE NO. 03

Diploma of Hospitality Management

A higher-level qualification for running hospitality operations: rostering, budgeting, service standards, and managing staff across front- and back-of-house.

Discipline: Hospitality

PLATE NO. 04

Certificate IV of Business

Core business skills for coordination and supervisory roles: workplace communication, basic project and finance administration, and business operations.

Discipline: Business

PLATE NO. 05

Diploma of Business

A deeper business qualification for those moving toward management: planning, marketing, finance, and leading teams within an organization.

Discipline: Business

PLATE NO. 06

Graduate Diploma of Management

An advanced qualification for experienced professionals building strategic management capability: organizational leadership, strategy, and governance.

Discipline: Management

PLATE NO. 07

General English Programs

English language programs supporting everyday communication and further study or work in Australia, for students building their English proficiency.

Discipline: English
